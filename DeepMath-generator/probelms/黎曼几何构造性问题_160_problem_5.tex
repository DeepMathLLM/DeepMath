problem:  
Let \( (M^{2n}, J, g_0) \) be a compact complex manifold admitting a **strong Kähler with torsion (SKT)** metric \( g_0 \), i.e. its fundamental form \( \omega_0 \) satisfies \( \partial\bar{\partial}\omega_0 = 0 \).  
The **pluriclosed flow**
\[
\frac{\partial \omega_t}{\partial t} = -(\rho^B_t)^{1,1}
\]
evolves \( \omega_t \) through SKT metrics. Streets–Tian have shown that this flow can be rewritten as part of a *generalized Ricci flow*, which admits local monotonicity formulas and weak analogues of entropy in certain low‑dimensional or homogeneous settings.

**Question:**  
Does there exist a globally defined, diffeomorphism‑invariant functional \( \mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{plc}} \) on the space of SKT metrics of a compact complex manifold such that:

1. The pluriclosed flow on the space of metrics is (up to diffeomorphism) the **gradient flow** of \( \mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{plc}} \) with respect to the natural \( L^2 \) Riemannian metric on the space of Hermitian forms,  
   \[
   \langle \dot\omega_1, \dot\omega_2 \rangle_\omega = \int_M \langle \dot\omega_1, \dot\omega_2 \rangle_\omega\, dV_\omega?
   \]
2. \( \mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{plc}} \) is **monotone decreasing** along any smooth solution \( \omega_t \) of the pluriclosed flow?  
3. The critical points of \( \mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{plc}} \) are precisely the **Bismut–Ricci‑flat SKT metrics** (i.e. those with \( (\rho^B)^{1,1}=0 \))?

Equivalently, is there a Perelman‑type entropy for pluriclosed flow valid for all compact SKT manifolds—one that extends the known partial entropy formulations from generalized Ricci flow and recovers pluriclosed flow as its \( L^2 \)‑gradient flow?

Why is it a "good" problem:  
This problem asks for a precise and universal analytical structure underlying pluriclosed flow—a global entropy or energy functional that governs its evolution and whose critical points correspond to canonical SKT metrics. It distills the question into the existence of a single geometric quantity with the same central role that Perelman’s \( \mathcal{W} \)‑entropy plays for Ricci flow.  

A positive answer would synthesize disparate approaches in complex non‑Kähler geometry, geometric PDE, and string‑inspired torsion geometry by giving a unified variational foundation for all SKT flows. It would illuminate long‑time behavior, stability, and convergence mechanisms for pluriclosed flow and potentially allow new monotonic quantities analogous to Perelman’s energies.  

The question is **simple to state yet deeply probing**, avoids ill‑defined terms, and bridges Hermitian complex geometry, geometric analysis, and flow theory. This blend of conceptual clarity, mathematical depth, and cross‑disciplinary relevance makes it a **good and meaningful research‑level problem**.
