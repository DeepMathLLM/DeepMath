problem:  
Let \((M,\Delta,g)\) be a compact, connected **contact sub-Riemannian manifold** of corank \(1\), with contact form \(\alpha\) and sub-Riemannian metric \(g\) on \(\Delta=\ker\alpha\). Let \(H(x,p)=\tfrac{1}{2}\langle p|_{\Delta_x},p|_{\Delta_x}\rangle_g\) denote the corresponding sub-Riemannian Hamiltonian, and \(\phi_t^H\) its Hamiltonian flow on \(T^*M\).  

Assume that on the energy level \(\Sigma:=H^{-1}(\tfrac{1}{2})\),
there exists a **free Hamiltonian \(S^1\)-action** preserving the symplectic structure and *commuting with* the Hamiltonian flow, i.e. 
\[
a\cdot \phi_t^H(p)=\phi_t^H(a\cdot p)\quad \text{for all } a\in S^1,\,p\in \Sigma.
\]
Suppose further that the quotient \(\Sigma/S^1\) is smooth and inherits a natural symplectic form for which the bundle projection \(\pi:\Sigma\to \Sigma/S^1\) is a principal circle bundle whose connection \(1\)-form is the restriction of the Liouville form \(\lambda_{\mathrm{can}}\).  

**Conjectural problem:**  
Must the sub-Riemannian structure \((M,\Delta,g)\), up to finite covering, arise from a **regular Sasakian manifold**, i.e. is there a contact form \(\tilde\alpha\) co-oriented with \(\alpha\) such that the Reeb vector field of \(\tilde\alpha\) generates the \(S^1\)-action and the transverse geometry of the quotient corresponds to a **Kähler manifold of constant holomorphic sectional curvature**?  
Equivalently, does the global existence of a Hamiltonian \(S^1\)-symmetry commuting with all sub-Riemannian geodesic flows enforce that the contact structure is regular and that the sub-Riemannian geometry is locally CR-symmetric?

Why is it a "good" problem:  
This problem corrects the structural mismatch between general higher-step sub-Riemannian systems and CR geometry, restricting to the canonical contact (step‑2) case where the Hamiltonian flow has deep geometric meaning. It focuses on the unexplored relation between **commuting Hamiltonian symmetries** and **contact/Sasakian rigidity**.  

- **Profound insight:** It asks whether continuous dynamical symmetry of the entire sub-Riemannian geodesic flow constrains the contact geometry so strictly that it forces a Sasakian model. This would extend the analogy between integrable geodesic dynamics and geometric regularity into the sub-Riemannian domain.  
- **Bridge between fields:** The problem intertwines **Hamiltonian dynamics**, **contact and symplectic topology**, and **Kähler geometry on the quotient**, forming a contact analogue of the symplectic reduction philosophy in mechanics.  
- **Simplicity and depth:** The hypothesis—an \(S^1\)-symmetry commuting with the geodesic flow—is easy to state but extremely restrictive, leading to implications cutting across differential geometry, Hamiltonian mechanics, and CR geometry.  
- **Potential impact:** A solution would describe new rigidity phenomena at the intersection of **sub-Riemannian geometry**, **contact symmetry**, and **complex curvature theory**, establishing potential classification results reminiscent of Zoll rigidity theorems but within a genuinely non-Riemannian setting.
