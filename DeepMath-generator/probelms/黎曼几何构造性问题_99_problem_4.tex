problem:  
Let \((M^n,g,f)\) be a complete, noncompact **gradient shrinking Ricci soliton** satisfying  
\[
\mathrm{Ric}_g + \nabla^2 f = \tfrac{1}{2} g,
\]
with normalization  
\[
(4\pi)^{-n/2}\int_M e^{-f}\,dV_g = 1.
\]  
Such triples \((M,g,f)\) are critical points of Perelman’s entropy functional  
\[
\mu(g,f) = \int_M \big(|\nabla f|^2 + R + f - n\big) (4\pi)^{-n/2} e^{-f}\,dV_g.
\]

Known examples (e.g., Gaussian shrinker, round cylinders, Bryant soliton) are expected to correspond to **isolated entropy critical points**, yet no analytic mechanism ensuring that isolation is understood.

**Problem:**  
Define the normalized moduli space of shrinkers (up to diffeomorphism and scaling)
\[
\mathcal{M}_n := \{(M,g,f): \mathrm{Ric}+\nabla^2 f=\tfrac12 g,\ (4\pi)^{-n/2}\int_M e^{-f}=1\}/\!\!\sim ,
\]
where \((M,g,f)\sim (M',g',f')\) if they differ by a diffeomorphism and scaling.

Focus on the **Perelman entropy functional** viewed as a map  
\[
\mu : \mathcal{M}_n \to \mathbb{R}.
\]

1. (**Local spectrum question**)  
Does the **Hessian of \(\mu\)** at a noncompact shrinker \((M,g,f)\) — interpreted as a self‑adjoint operator on symmetric 2‑tensors with respect to the weighted measure \(e^{-f}dV_g\) — have **discrete spectrum** near zero?  
Equivalently, do infinitesimal deformations of a shrinker split into finitely many entropy‑neutral directions and a discrete positive spectrum?

2. (**Entropy accumulation question**)  
Is it possible to construct a sequence of nonhomothetic shrinkers \((M_i,g_i,f_i)\) with uniformly bounded curvature such that  
\[
\mu(g_i,f_i)\to \mu(g_\infty,f_\infty)
\]
for some limit shrinker \((M_\infty,g_\infty,f_\infty)\),
while the corresponding eigenvalues of the Hessian at each point display **accumulation from above** at \(0\)?  
In other words, can entropy‑criticality fail to be spectrally isolated even if geometric convergence holds?

Why is it a "good" problem:  
This formulation converts the vague “entropy discreteness” intuition into a precise **analytic spectral** problem: does the Hessian of Perelman’s entropy on the moduli space of shrinkers possess discrete spectrum near zero, or can there exist continuous families of nearly entropy‑neutral deformations?  
The question probes the **stability structure** of Ricci solitons from a variational viewpoint and links **Ricci flow stability theory**, **nonlinear elliptic analysis**, and **weighted spectral geometry**.  
A positive answer would establish that Perelman’s entropy endows the moduli space of shrinkers with a **Morse–Smale type structure**, allowing an “energy landscape” picture of Ricci singularity models.  
Conversely, a negative answer would identify new genuinely continuous moduli phenomena for self‑similar solutions of Ricci flow.  
Its statement is clear and concise yet demands a deep synthesis of geometry, analysis, and spectral theory — precisely the kind of insight-driven and cross‑field challenge that defines a good research problem.
