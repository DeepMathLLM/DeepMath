problem:  
Let \((X,d,\mathfrak{m})\) be a compact metric measure space satisfying the RCD\((K,N)\) condition for some \(K>0\), \(1<N<\infty\). For each \(p\in X\), consider the distance function \(r_p(x)=d(p,x)\), whose Laplacian \(\Delta r_p\) is known in general to be a measure satisfying a distributional comparison inequality
\[
\Delta r_p \le (N-1)\frac{\mathrm{sn}'_K(r_p)}{\mathrm{sn}_K(r_p)}\,\mathfrak{m}
\]
outside \(p\).  
It is further known that if \((X,d,\mathfrak{m})\) is a smooth Riemannian manifold with \(\mathrm{Ric}\ge K\), the same inequality holds pointwise almost everywhere outside the cut locus.  

**Open problem (rigidity via pointwise comparison):**  
Suppose that for every \(p\in X\) there exists a Borel representative of \(\Delta r_p\) that satisfies the Laplacian comparison inequality pointwise \(\mathfrak{m}\)-almost everywhere on \(X\setminus\{p\}\).  

Does this property imply that \((X,d,\mathfrak{m})\) is, up to a set of \(\mathfrak{m}\)-measure zero, isomorphic as a metric measure space to a smooth Riemannian manifold satisfying \(\mathrm{Ric}\ge K\)?  

Conversely, is the pointwise Laplacian comparison property equivalent to the existence of a \(C^{2}\)-Riemannian structure compatible with \((d,\mathfrak{m})\) among RCD\((K,N)\) spaces?  

Why is it a "good" problem:  
This version gives a precise rigidity question at the intersection of analysis on metric measure spaces and differential geometry. The Laplacian comparison is a central analytic manifestation of lower Ricci curvature, but in the synthetic RCD setting it is only known in a weak measure sense—an echo of degeneracy at singular points. Asking whether the strengthening to a pointwise form forces the space to be smooth seeks to identify an intrinsic analytic criterion for regularity.  

Conceptually, it directly challenges our understanding of how far synthetic curvature bounds approximate the smooth world, testing whether the analytic behavior of distance functions captures full geometric smoothness. It bridges PDE-type analytic structures (Laplacian comparison) with global geometric regularity (smooth manifold structure). Resolving this would yield a new analytic characterization of smooth Ricci-lower-bounded manifolds within the RCD universe, advancing both geometric analysis and metric measure theory.
