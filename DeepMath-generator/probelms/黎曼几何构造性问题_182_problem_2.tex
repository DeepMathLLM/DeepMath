problem:  
Let \((M,\mathcal{D},g)\) be a **compact, step-2 equiregular sub-Riemannian manifold** equipped with its canonical Popp measure \(\mu\).  Assume that \((M,\mathcal{D},g)\) is *not* contact, so that its horizontal distribution is bracket-generating of step 2 but lacks a global contact form.  Let \(\exp_x:T_x^*M\to M\) denote the sub-Riemannian exponential map generated by the Hamiltonian \(H=\frac12 g^{-1}\).  Write
\[
J(x,\lambda,t) := \det_{\mathcal{D}} ( D_\lambda \exp_x(t\lambda) )
\]
for the horizontal Jacobian of the exponential at \(x\) along normal extremals, computed using the canonical frame of Agrachev–Barilari–Rizzi (when it exists).  In the contact setting, ABR curvature theory shows that the \(t^2\) coefficient in \(-\log J\) is a combination of canonical curvature invariants, and specifically encodes \(\mathrm{Ric}_{\mathrm{ABR}}\).

**Problem:**  
In the non-contact, step-2 equiregular setting—where abnormal minimizers may co-exist with normal extremals—does there exist an **intrinsic analytic invariant** \(\mathrm{Ric}_\mathrm{eff}(\lambda)\) determined solely by the small-time asymptotics of the transport-induced density distortion
\[
-\log \frac{\det_{\mathcal{D}}\!(D_\lambda \exp_x(t\lambda))}{t^{Q-1}}\!,
\quad Q=\dim_H(M),
\]
such that:
1. \(\mathrm{Ric}_\mathrm{eff}=\mathrm{Ric}_{\mathrm{ABR}}\) in the contact case, and  
2. \(\mathrm{Ric}_\mathrm{eff}\) still governs the infinitesimal convexity of entropy along normal Wasserstein-geodesics induced by the Hamiltonian flow?

In other words, can one define a **synthetic Ricci curvature via volume distortion** in general step-2 sub-Riemannian manifolds that both extends ABR curvature and describes the analytic measure-contraction geometry near normal geodesics, even when the contact structure (and hence the canonical frame) degenerates?

Why is it a "good" problem:  
- **Conceptual depth:** The question aims to generalize the known ABR curvature–Jacobian expansion relation beyond contact manifolds into the more intricate step-2 world where abnormal geodesics and mixed-type distortions occur, posing a new challenge not addressed by existing theory.  
- **Bridge between frameworks:** It builds an explicit bridge among three components that remain disconnected: (i) ABR geometric curvature, (ii) measure-contraction/optimal transport asymptotics, and (iii) analytic invariants of sub-Riemannian volume distortion.  
- **True novelty:** Whereas the ABR expansion is settled for contact structures, the existence of a unified curvature invariant derived from Jacobian asymptotics in non-contact step-2 geometries is completely open and central for any extension of the curvature–dimension condition \(CD(K,N)\) to general sub-Riemannian spaces.  
- **Simplicity and universality:** The question asks, in essence, “Does small-time volume distortion always remember an intrinsic Ricci curvature in sub-Riemannian manifolds?”—a short, intelligible statement that probes deeply into geometric analysis and control theory.  
- **Potential impact:** Its resolution would identify the correct notion of *effective Ricci curvature* governing measure contraction in all step-2 sub-Riemannian geometries, thereby enabling a consistent generalization of synthetic curvature bounds beyond the contact realm, influencing optimal transport, heat flow, and analysis on metric-measure sub-Riemannian spaces.
